Compress idle timeline - 4 options

Compare all options (tabs)

  • Option A · Broken axis
    Keeps true clock axis and obvious idle markers; hard 30m threshold, ticks jump across breaks.
  • Option B · Square-root scale
    One continuous axis, no thresholds; early blips still near-invisible and gaps still dominate.
  • Option C · Two zones + zoom
    Best for the active window and keeps whole-day context; uses two bars, more vertical space.
  • Option D · Work blocks only
    Most readable for what-was-done; loses proportional sense of time of day.
